# BTDeck Privacy Policy

_Effective 25 September 2026_

BTDeck is made by Divyanshu Lakra. This policy explains what the BTDeck Android app
(`dev.btdeck`) does with your information.

**In short: BTDeck does not collect, send or sell any of your data.** The app has no internet
access, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. Everything it keeps stays on
your phone.

## What stays on your phone

To work, BTDeck saves these things in its own private storage on your phone:

- **Your layouts**: the buttons you make, their names, colours and shortcuts.
- **Your settings**, such as button style, vibration and spacing.
- **Images you add** to buttons. BTDeck only sees the images you pick in the Android photo
  picker, and keeps its own copy of each one. It can't see the rest of your photos.
- **The last device you connected to** (its Bluetooth address), so BTDeck can reconnect to it.

None of this leaves your phone through BTDeck. If Android backup is turned on for your phone,
Android may include BTDeck's data in your own Google backup, like it does for other apps. You
control this in your phone's settings.

Text you type in the keyboard screen is sent straight to your connected device and isn't
saved.

## Bluetooth and the permissions BTDeck asks for

BTDeck connects to your tablet or computer as a Bluetooth keyboard. The keys you press go
only to the device you connected to, over Bluetooth.

- **Nearby devices (Bluetooth)**: to find, pair and connect to your tablet or computer, and to
  send it key presses. BTDeck shows the names of nearby Bluetooth devices so you can pick
  one. It doesn't save or share that list.
- **Location** (only on Android 11 and older): older versions of Android require this
  permission to search for Bluetooth devices. BTDeck never reads your location.
- **Notifications**: to show that BTDeck is connected while it runs in the background.

## Sharing layouts

If you choose to share a layout, BTDeck makes a `.keyset` file with that layout and its
images, and hands it to the app you pick (for example, a messaging app). That app's own
privacy policy then applies. BTDeck never shares anything unless you ask it to.

## Deleting your data

You can delete layouts and images inside the app. To remove everything, clear BTDeck's
storage in your phone's settings, or uninstall the app.

## Children

BTDeck doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective
